This dataset contains 7 transects associated with the shoreline at site nzd0023 . Each transect represents a segment of the coastline for which elevation and positional data have been analysed to estimate shoreline change trends and related metrics. The orientation of transects spans from 66.957 ° to 82.266 °, with a mean orientation of 73.827 °, indicating the general alignment of shoreline segments within this site.

In terms of shoreline change:

  • The average linear trend across all transects is 0.15 m/year, with individual values ranging from 0.1 to 0.185 , suggesting a net accretional trend overall.

  • The Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) of the trend model averages 9.653 m, indicating the typical deviation between observed points and the fitted trend line.

Confidence intervals on shoreline position trends are also available:

  • The mean lower bound is 0.059 , and the mean upper bound is 0.074 , reflecting the uncertainty range around trend estimates.

The number of valid data points per transect ( n_points_nonan ) ranges from 205 to 218 , with an average of 211.857 .
